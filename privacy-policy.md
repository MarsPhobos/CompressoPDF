Privacy Policy – CompressoPDF

Effective date: 12 Feb. 2026 

CompressoPDF respects your privacy and is designed to work primarily offline.

PDF Processing

All PDF compression and editing happens locally on your device.
Your files are never uploaded, stored, or shared with any server.

Data Collection

CompressoPDF does not directly collect, store, or transmit personal information.

However, the app uses third-party services that may automatically collect limited technical data.

Advertising

This app uses Google AdMob to display ads.

AdMob may automatically collect:

Device identifiers (Advertising ID)

Device information

App usage and interaction data

Crash and performance diagnostics

This information is used for:

Showing ads

Measuring ad performance

Fraud prevention

Improving service reliability

Google handles this data according to its Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can reset or disable your Advertising ID in your device settings.

Permissions

Internet – required to load ads

File access – required to open and save PDFs

Children

CompressoPDF is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children’s data.

Your Control

You may:

Use the app offline (except ads)

Reset your Advertising ID

Disable personalized ads from device settings
